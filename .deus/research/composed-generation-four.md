# Outcome

Independent bundled fragments now cover all four workflows through real maintained emission and runtime. A fresh cohort passed four supplied semantic checks plus the additional late-window check. **Latency regressed: 80.116 seconds versus the 69.522-second DEAL 1.4 baseline (+15.2%).** This is not completion of the unchanged objective.

## What changed

A single native `submit_compact` accepts actual nested `{title,fragments,commands,display}`. Inputs, reads, choices, allocations, exclusive stock-qualified alternatives, authoritative quotes, comparisons and results instantiate separately with namespaced state/actions/views. Pure formulas connect actual typed ports. Commands derive prerequisite execution and downstream local initialization; model-authored workflow graphs are not required. Per-resource ownership, stale-completion fencing, optional presence, per-reply expiry, canonical exact-edit recovery and explicit custom/full-source paths remain.

No discovery, provider-authority or public-protocol replacement was introduced. Providers retain records, authorization, business validation and whole-basket hire/deposit prices. No extra selector/verifier call was added.
