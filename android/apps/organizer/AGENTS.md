# Organizer assistance

Follow the frozen contract in `.deus/design/organizer-assistance.md`. Initiate assistance through an event requirement, never a workspace or generation-mode launcher. Use task language on ordinary screens; Inspect is the single entrance for engineering taxonomy, source, provenance and unified activity.

Use shared AssistanceDesign/Material presentation rules. Show actionable connection/timeout/rejection explanations rather than exception names or source-attempt counts on ordinary screens; keep exact gateway codes and causes in Inspect. Disable conflicting actions and edits while their real operation is pending; show truthful progress and restore controls on completion/failure/cancellation. Keep Inspect and appropriate cancellation available. No inert buttons or simulated operational results.

Publish a fresh ShowState identity for plan edits; do not mutate the previously rendered plan object. Call ExperienceRuntime serially. Preserve accepted source and the previous requirement link on failed replacement. Requirement identity includes exact quantity/specification/period; never infer links or authorship from saved source/title. Route decisions remain in DEAL.

Bind local review records to the native invoking workspace, not a global latest-record guess. Durable earlier preparation must not expose stale review on cold mount; restore review only for a still-current live session or a new staging reference. Requirement-linked shell navigation must not duplicate retained content's task heading. Apply Inspect's model-event limit after model filtering, not after unrelated provider/action events. Preparation never establishes booking or coverage. Preserve exact provider arguments, consent and machine timestamps; shorten presentation only. Keep raw diagnostics in Inspect without hiding actionable operational errors.

Clearly separate test-only authored source/frozen UI states from live model evidence. Extend renderer widgets only for demonstrated task needs, with typed contracts and actual compiler/sandbox/device checks.
