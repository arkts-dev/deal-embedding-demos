# Organizer assistance

Follow the frozen contract in `.deus/design/organizer-assistance.md`. Initiate assistance through an event requirement, never a workspace or generation-mode launcher. Use task language on ordinary screens; Inspect is the single entrance for engineering taxonomy, source, provenance and unified activity.

Use shared AssistanceDesign/Material presentation rules. Disable conflicting actions and edits while their real operation is pending; show truthful progress and restore controls on completion/failure/cancellation. Keep Inspect and appropriate cancellation available. No inert buttons or simulated operational results.

Call ExperienceRuntime serially. Preserve accepted source and the previous requirement link on failed replacement. Requirement identity includes exact quantity/specification/period; never infer links or authorship from saved source/title. Route decisions remain in DEAL.

Bind local review records to the native invoking workspace, not a global latest-record guess. Preparation never establishes booking or coverage. Preserve exact provider arguments, consent and machine timestamps; shorten presentation only. Keep raw diagnostics in Inspect without hiding actionable operational errors.

Clearly separate test-only authored source/frozen UI states from live model evidence. Extend renderer widgets only for demonstrated task needs, with typed contracts and actual compiler/sandbox/device checks.
